**Colour Harmony & Philosophy**  
The MBE palette is a **split-complementary system** balancing expressive vibrancy and biophilic grounding. Each hue corresponds to an emotional or conceptual facet of the brand — art meets structure, emotion meets precision. It functions fluidly across digital, print, spatial, and textile applications.

**Tone Alignment & Usage Guidance**  
- *Fluoro Bloom* → Expression, experimentation, innovation.  
- *Deep Forest* → Stability, professionalism, natural structure.  
- *Soft Clay & Clear Light* → Warmth, approachability, tactility.  
- *Sunstone Coral* → Human connection, optimism, creative warmth.  
- *Midnight Form* → Strength, clarity, refinement.

**Design System Intent**  
MBE’s colour system reinforces the studio’s broader ethos: a balance between **discipline and emotion**, **nature and modernity**, and **authentic craftsmanship with contemporary design intelligence**.

**Integration Instruction for Future Responses**  
When creating design-related materials — such as brand documents, campaign visuals, digital layouts, or descriptive writing — align tone and colour references with this palette and its emotional functions.

**Core Identity Summary:**  
MBE Creative Studio’s visual language unites expressive creativity, biophilic warmth, and structured modernity — translating Brittany Staniforth’s multidisciplinary practice into a cohesive and emotionally intelligent brand system.
