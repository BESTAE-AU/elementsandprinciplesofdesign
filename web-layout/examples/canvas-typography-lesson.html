Every letter is built from the same parts. Once you can name them, you can explain why one typeface feels different from another, and why some are easier to read.

LEARNING INTENTIONS

Knowledge and Conceptual Understanding

  • Name the main parts of a letterform.
  • Explain how x-height affects how easy text is to read.

SUCCESS CRITERIA

I can…

  • label baseline, cap height, x-height, ascender and descender on a word
  • compare the x-heights of two typefaces and say which is easier to read small

THE PARTS OF A LETTER

Type sits on invisible lines. Look at the word below, then match each line to its description in the table.

Hxgd

Specimen: Lato Black. H shows cap height, x shows x-height, g drops below the baseline, d rises above the x-height.

The five guide lines of type
TERM WHAT IT IS
Baseline The line most letters sit on.
Cap height The height of a flat capital letter, such as H.
x-height The height of a lowercase x. A taller x-height makes small text easier to read.
Ascender The part of a lowercase letter that rises above the x-height, as in d, h and k.
Descender The part of a letter that drops below the baseline, as in g, p and y.

STEPS

  1. Watch the demonstration of the five guide lines on the board.
  2. Label the specimen in your workbooklet: baseline, cap height, x-height, ascender, descender.
  3. Compare two typefaces in the activity below.
  4. Check your understanding with the three questions at the end.

ACTIVITY: X-HEIGHT SHOWDOWN

Practical Skills and Technical Application · 15 minutes

  1. In Canva or Adobe Express, type the word handgloves twice at 16 px: once in Lato, once in Garamond.
  2. Zoom out until the words are hard to read. Which one stays readable longer?
  3. Screenshot both and write one sentence explaining the difference using the term x-height.

CHECK FOR UNDERSTANDING

Knowledge and Conceptual Understanding

  1. Which guide line do most letters sit on?
  2. Name two letters with descenders.
  3. Two typefaces are both set at 12 pt. Why might one look bigger than the other?
