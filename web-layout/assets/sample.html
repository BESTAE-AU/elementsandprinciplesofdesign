Elements of design

Layout on the web

Every module, gutter and margin is a whole 5 px step. The grid is built from the margins inwards, so twelve columns fill the live area exactly.

Heading one runs across two lines at most

Body text is 18 px Lato Regular on 25 px leading, which is 139 %, close to the house 135 %. Every line lands on the 5 px baseline, and paragraphs are spaced by one full line.

Headings are ALL CAPS Lato Black, sized from their cap height in 5 px steps. Their blocks are padded so the next item starts on a row line.
