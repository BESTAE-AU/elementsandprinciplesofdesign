This is the brand of Marist Catholic College North Shore (MCCNS). The guideline's letterhead example lists St Mary's K–6 (40 Ridge Street) and La Valla 7–12 (270 Miller Street), North Sydney. This system restates the college's **Visual Identity Guidelines (November 2020)** exactly and adds usage rules for combining the colours legibly. Never alter the brand: no new colours, no recoloured or redrawn logos, no substitute typefaces beyond those the guideline allows.

## Content fundamentals

Write the college name in full as **Marist Catholic College North Shore**; never type it out as a stand-in for the logo. Use Australian English. The guideline sets no voice or tone rules. Follow the school's own communications style, and for classroom resources follow the teacher's resource style (e.g. the BESTAE system), inside the MCCNS frame described below.

## Visual foundations: colour

The palette is two primary and two secondary colours, and nothing else: `mcc-navy` and `mcc-light-blue` (primary), `mcc-yellow` and `mcc-cerise` (secondary), on `surface` (white). The navy family carries the brand and the two warm secondaries are accents.

- **Proportion (60-30-10):** `surface` and `mcc-light-blue` about 60% (grounds, panels), `mcc-navy` about 30% (headings, text, bars), and **one** of `mcc-yellow` or `mcc-cerise` about 10% per page or slide (highlights, key figures, the spear). Save yellow and cerise together for the crest and major event material.
- **Default text** is `ink` (`mcc-navy`) on `surface` or `mcc-light-blue`. `black` is fine for Word documents and letters.
- **On `mcc-navy`:** `on-dark` for body text, `mcc-yellow` for headings and highlights.
- **On `mcc-cerise`:** `on-dark`. `mcc-yellow` only for bold headings (4.7:1).
- **On `mcc-yellow`:** `mcc-navy` only.
- **`mcc-light-blue` is a ground, never a text colour.** White, yellow or cerise text on it fails.
- **Never set `mcc-cerise` and `mcc-navy` on each other** (2.4:1). Where the two blocks meet, separate them with white or yellow, as the crest does.
- **Never set `mcc-yellow` text on `surface`** (1.5:1).
- Navy and cerise both print as dark greys, so label anything colour-coded (tabs, charts, houses, year groups) with words as well.
- **Which value to use:** CMYK for professional print, PMS (282 C, 116 C, 220 C) for uniforms, merchandise and signage, RGB/HEX for Word, PowerPoint, Canva and the web. Take print values from the tokens' notes, never convert them from screen RGB. On-screen yellow can't be matched exactly in CMYK.
- The 2020 PDF prints Light Blue's HEX as `#D9DDE2`. Its RGB, CMYK and drawn swatch are all `#909DB2`, which is the value used here.

## Visual foundations: typography

- **Gotham** is the primary family for all external and professionally designed material. Use `heading` and `subheading` (Gotham Medium) and `body` (Gotham Book).
- **Garamond Semibold** (`major-heading`) is for major headings only, in ALL CAPITALS with 100 tracking. Use it sparingly.
- **Helvetica** (`office-heading`, `office-subheading`, `office-body`) is for internal work where Gotham isn't available: Word, PowerPoint, letters and emails. **Never use Helvetica on external communications** such as signage or brochures.
- **Spacing rules from the guideline:** headings and subheadings may be all capitals or upper and lower case. Subheads are 2 pt above subhead copy size. Body copy is left aligned, with leading 2 pt above body size. The guideline sets no absolute sizes, so the sizes here assume a 10 pt body; scale them together.
- No font files are included. Gotham is a licensed typeface, so use the college's installed copy.

## Logo and crest

Use only the supplied files (see the Logos asset group and the original guideline PDF in Guidelines). The official artwork is the `.eps` suite named in the guideline, e.g. `North Shore MCC CMYK VERTICAL.eps`. The PNGs here are copies taken from the guideline PDF for reference and on-screen drafts. Request the `.eps` files for print.

- **Versions:** standard vertical, standard horizontal, crest only, reversed (white, yellow or full colour), and one-colour mono (cerise PMS 220, navy PMS 282, black or white).
- **Grounds:** the standard logo on `surface` or `mcc-light-blue`. Reversed logos only on dark grounds, preferably `mcc-navy`. Never on busy photographic or patterned backgrounds.
- **Clear space:** the height of the letter "M" on all sides.
- **Minimum sizes:** `logo-min-horizontal` (57 mm), `logo-min-vertical` (20 mm), `crest-min` (20 mm).
- **Never:** type the name in place of the logo, change its proportions or elements, stretch it, tilt it, combine landscape and portrait elements, or change its colours.

## Graphic elements

- **Mary's Monogram** may be used as a corner graphic or background image, always at 100% proportion. It may be used at reduced opacity as a background.
- **The spear** is a divider between text blocks.
- Both are supplied as cropped PNG (for direct placement) or EPS with bleed (for external print). Neither file is included here yet.

## Sydney Catholic Schools endorsed logo

All external marketing material carries the SCS endorsed logo, bottom right or bottom centre, front or back.
- It appears in black, or reversed white out of a colour strip, at **50% of the school logo's size**.
- Minimums are `scs-logo-min-horizontal` (40 mm) and `scs-logo-min-vertical` (20 mm).
- Alongside a differentiated SCS service logo (e.g. Newman Selective Gifted Education), the two logos are equal in size, both in black or both reversed white.

## Using MCCNS with a teacher's resource system

In classroom resources, MCCNS is the **frame** and the teaching system is the **content**:
- The logo, header and footer bars, and document or cover titles use MCCNS tokens and Helvetica (Word, PowerPoint) or Gotham (designed material).
- Domain or activity colour-coding (e.g. BESTAE's H/S/T domain colours) stays inside the page and follows its own contrast rules.
- Don't use a colour that could pass for an MCCNS colour. BESTAE `h1-jazzberry` (`#AC145A`) is almost identical to `mcc-cerise` (1.02:1), so leave it out of MCCNS-framed work.

## Iconography

The guideline defines no icon set. Where an icon is needed, use a simple single-weight line icon in `mcc-navy` and flag it as a placeholder.
