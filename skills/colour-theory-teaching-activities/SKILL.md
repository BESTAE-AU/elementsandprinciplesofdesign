# Colour theory teaching activities

## Pick the right activity

1. Identify the **subject, stage and outcome type**:
   - *Know or understand* (the science, vocabulary).
   - *Apply or decide* (build a palette, choose a colour for a brief).
   - *Make* (dye, print, digital file).
   - *Evaluate or analyse* (poster analysis, brand critique).
2. Choose from `references/activities.md` and adapt it. Each activity lists the concept, source video, materials, steps and an extension.
3. Pair each activity with **one short video** from `references/video-map.md`. Prefer the shortest video that covers the concept, and give the segment ("first 3 minutes"). Flag that Flux Academy's *How to Choose Colors* (KMS) has **no usable transcript**, so preview it before use.
4. Add **retrieval and checking:** a hinge question, 3 quick-recall questions, and a success criterion ("I can explain why screens use RGB").
5. Differentiate:
   - **Support:** colour wheel templates, word banks (hue, value, saturation, tint, shade, tone, warm/cool) and sentence stems ("The designer used ___ which creates ___ because ___").
   - **Extension:** hex maths by hand, auditing a real website's contrast, explaining metamerism with spectral curves.

## Suggested sequence (about 6 lessons, adjust per course)

| # | Focus | Core activity | Video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What colour is, how we see it | Coloured-shadow demo, then the red + green = yellow puzzle | l8 (3.5 min), -b2 demo |
| 2 | Additive vs subtractive, colour codes | RGB vs CMYK sort. Hex maths challenge | TGc (2 min), llA segment |
| 3 | Colour vocabulary and harmonies | Wheel build with tints, shades and tones, then poster harmony hunt | YeI (6.5 min), x0s |
| 4 | Balance and accessibility | 60-30-10 ratio bar. Contrast audit of a real web page | V-SD (2 min), oDn |
| 5 | Psychology and branding | Brand case study (McDonald's vs Pepsi 2009), then justify a colour for a student brand | WjQ, mpK |
| 6 | Apply | Build a palette for a brief using the 7-step process, then present the rationale | Co75 (selected segments) |

Subject tailoring:
- **Textiles:** metamerism light box, pigment vs dye, dye-matching.
- **Multimedia:** RGB/hex, screen contrast, colour grading ("subtractive look").
- **Graphics:** CMYK/Pantone, brand deck colour slides.
- **Visual Arts:** simultaneous contrast (Van Gogh), afterimages, colour constancy illusions.
