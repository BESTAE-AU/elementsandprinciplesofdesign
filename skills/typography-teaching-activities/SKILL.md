# Typography teaching activities

## Pick the right activity

1. Identify the **subject, stage and outcome type**:
   - *Know or understand* (vocabulary, anatomy, history).
   - *Apply or decide* (pick fonts for a brief, build a hierarchy).
   - *Make* (a poster, type system, logotype, web page).
   - *Evaluate or analyse* (critique a poster, analyse a brand's type).
2. Choose from `references/activities.md` and adapt it. Each activity lists the concept, source video, materials, steps and an extension.
3. Pair each activity with **one short video segment** from `references/video-map.md`. Give the timestamps ("DSP 7:02–10:04 for weight, baseline and leading"), not the whole video.
4. Add **retrieval and checking**: a hinge question, 3 quick-recall questions, and a success criterion ("I can explain the difference between kerning and tracking").
5. Differentiate:
   - **Support:** labelled anatomy diagrams, word banks (serif, sans serif, x-height, baseline, leading, tracking, kerning, hierarchy, weight) and sentence stems ("The designer used a ___ typeface, which suggests ___ because ___").
   - **Extension:** build a type scale with `type-system-builder`, kern a logotype by hand, research a typeface designer, write CSS with rem and ch units.

## Suggested sequence (about 6 lessons, adjust per course)

| # | Focus | Core activity | Video |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | What typography is; typeface vs font; tone of voice | Tone-of-voice match-up, then the bank logotype vote | CAN 0:05–3:13; ENV 1:01–2:03 |
| 2 | Classifications and personality | Category sort; a history timeline including "sans is older than serif?" | CAN 4:14–6:21; DSP 1:00–5:02; ENV 2:03–7:08 |
| 3 | Anatomy and units | Anatomy labelling on handwriting-paper lines; pt/px/rem conversion challenge | DSP 5:02–9:03 |
| 4 | Spacing: tracking, kerning, leading, line length | Kerning game; the line length and leading experiment | CAN 6:21–8:28; DSP 9:03–11:05 |
| 5 | Hierarchy and type systems | Squint test critique; build paragraph styles on a scale | CAN 3:13–4:14; DSP 11:05–13:07 |
| 6 | Apply: pairing for a brief | Heading/body pairing challenge, then a written rationale | CPS 0:00–4:05; CAN 8:28–10:33 |

Subject tailoring:
- **Graphics Technology:** posters, packaging, logotypes, InDesign paragraph styles, print units (pt, pica, mm).
- **Multimedia:** web type in rem and ch, title cards, motion typography, contrast for screens.
- **Visual Arts / Visual Design:** type as image, expressive typography, analysing posters and book covers (the frames and conceptual framework).
- **Textiles / D&T:** labels, swing tags, brand identity for a product, legibility on small printed items.
