# Font library and personality guide

Source codes: CAN = Canva *Understanding Typography*, DSP = DesignSpo *Ultimate Guide to Typography*, ENV = Envato Tuts+ *Psychology of Fonts*, CPS = The Color Palette Studio *My Favorite Body Fonts*. Fonts marked "(added)" are well-known free options added for coverage; they are not from the videos.

## 1. Personality by category (ENV, DSP, CAN)

| Category | Keywords | Typical clients | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serif | Stability, tradition, intellect, formality, enduring value, luxury | Banks, law firms, universities, jewellers, publishers, luxury | Can feel old-fashioned for playful or tech briefs |
| Slab serif | Strong, enduring, powerful, brash | Car brands, outdoors and camping, workwear | Heavy; use for headings |
| Sans serif | Progressive, clean, open, friendly, neutral, versatile | Tech (Facebook, Apple, Spotify), retail, government, signage | Can look generic; many brands end up looking the same (DSP) |
| Geometric / modernist sans | Luxury, elegance, modern precision | Fragrance, fashion, high-end products | Very round forms can reduce body-text legibility |
| Rounded sans | Informal, soft, youthful | Kids' products, friendly apps | Can seem unserious for formal briefs |
| Formal script | Romantic, formal, luxurious | Weddings, fine dining, luxury | Unreadable in all caps or at small sizes |
| Casual script / handwritten | Friendly, personal, fun | Birthday cards, kids' clothing, cafés | Can look amateur; use sparingly |
| Display | Whatever the theme needs (Western, cosmic, retro) | Titles, logos, posters | Never for body copy, buttons or labels (DSP) |
| Monospace | Technical, coding, retro-computer | Tech, data, developer tools | Wide and uneven for long reading |

Judge scripts by their strokes: long, flowing calligraphic strokes feel classy, while chunky informal ones feel casual (ENV).

## 2. Body fonts (CPS unless marked)

### Free (Google Fonts)

| Font | Type | Character | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Poppins | Geometric sans | Round, bouncy, youthful; wide (takes horizontal space) | Friendly digital brands |
| Public Sans | Sans | Narrow, very readable; fits lots of copy | Copy-heavy sites, reports |
| Raleway | Sans | Elegant; distinctive crossed W and bouncy numerals | Lifestyle, creative brands (check numerals suit the use) |
| DM Sans | Geometric sans | Classic, mature, never dramatic; sister of DM Serif | All-rounder; superfamily pairing |
| Libre Baskerville | Transitional serif | Romantic, graceful, flowing; classic numerals | Editorial, weddings, boutique brands |
| Merriweather | Serif | Sharp flat serifs, upright "good posture"; built for screens | Long reading on screen |
| Noto Serif | Serif | Designed by Google for legibility; narrow, no-nonsense | Multilingual, long text |
| EB Garamond | Old-style serif | Classic, nostalgic, a little retro | Books, heritage, literary |
| IBM Plex Serif | Serif | Tall, narrow, "stands up straight"; pairs with Plex Sans | Tech-meets-editorial |
| Lato (added) | Humanist sans | Warm, stable, many weights | Workbooklets (house font), education |
| Inter (added) | Sans | Designed for UI screens; large x-height | Apps, dashboards |
| Source Serif / Source Sans (added) | Superfamily | Neutral and very legible | Documents, reports |
| Atkinson Hyperlegible (added) | Sans | Designed by the Braille Institute for low-vision readers | Accessibility-first work |
| Lexend (added) | Sans | Designed to reduce reading strain | Support materials, younger readers |

### Premium (CPS)

| Font | Character | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Apercu | Warm, bouncy, modern, youthful; distinctive lowercase a | CPS says it's used by BarkBox, Glossier and Chick-fil-A |
| Proxima Nova | No-nonsense classic sans | A premium cousin of free sans serifs |
| Essential Sans | Rounded, approachable, soft, modern | A premium alternative to Poppins |
| Avenir | Geometric sans with a short x-height ("stocky") | Timeless |
| Helvetica | Tight, tall, neutral neo-grotesque | One of the best-selling fonts. Free lookalike: Arimo, or Inter for screens |

Narrow faces fit more copy per line (Public Sans, Noto Serif, IBM Plex Serif, Helvetica). Round, wide faces feel friendlier but need more space (Poppins).

## 3. Proven free pairings

| Heading | Body | Strategy | Mood |
|---|---|---|---|
| DM Serif Display | DM Sans | Superfamily | Classic-modern, polished |
| Playfair Display | Source Sans 3 | Contrasting | Editorial, elegant |
| Montserrat (bold) | Merriweather | Contrasting | Modern headline, readable long text |
| Poppins (bold) | Poppins (regular) | One family | Friendly, youthful, simple |
| Roboto Slab | Roboto | Superfamily | Sturdy, practical |
| Abril Fatface | Lato | Contrasting (display + humanist) | Bold magazine feel |
| Libre Baskerville (bold/caps) | Libre Baskerville | One family | Refined, literary |
| Bebas Neue | Open Sans | Same category (condensed caps + neutral sans) | Sporty, poster |
| IBM Plex Sans | IBM Plex Serif | Superfamily | Technical, trustworthy |
| Pacifico (logo only) | Nunito | Contrasting (script + rounded sans) | Casual café, kids |

Check that each font is available in the tool the user works in before recommending it.
