# Value: analysis and model answers

Use this file for analyses, annotations, exemplars and model answers about value in a specific design.

## Workflow

1. **Fix the artefact.** Work from the user's image or description. If you're inventing one for an exemplar, describe it briefly first so students can picture it, with the specific value characteristics the answers will cite.
2. **Work through the progression** (`method/analysis.md`): identify → describe → explain → analyse (connect to communication, purpose, audience) → evaluate against a named criterion, including a limitation or trade-off.
3. **For levelled exemplars** (e.g. developing / sound / high-range), make each level add a link in the reasoning chain, not just more words. Respect word limits. Under each, add a short note on what lifts it to the next level. The justification ladder in `feedback.md` shows the pattern.
4. **Go beyond the element on its own.** Use the relationships with other elements and principles in `knowledge.md`.
5. **Language** (`method/checks-and-language.md`): conditional wording for emotional or cultural readings, observation before interpretation, and no "it works" without a criterion.


## Worked analysis (model for exemplars and annotations)

Scenario: a low-key product photo for a candle brand. The background is near-black; the candle is lit from the left by its own flame and a small side light; the brand name sits in pale cream text in the lower right.

- **Identify:** "The photo uses value."
- **Describe:** "Most of the frame is very dark; the brightest values are the flame and the lit left side of the candle; the right side falls into shadow; the cream text is the second-lightest area."
- **Explain:** "Because almost everything is dark, the small bright flame becomes the focal point, and the gradient across the candle shows it's a rounded form."
- **Analyse:** "Value works with form and emphasis: the light-to-shadow transition models the cylinder, and the low-key range leaves only two light areas — flame then text — so the eye moves from product to brand name. The narrow bright range may suggest warmth and intimacy in this context."
- **Evaluate:** "Effective for a premium website hero image. However, the dark shadows may block up and lose the candle's texture when printed on uncoated stock for flyers; a print-adjusted version with lifted shadows should be proofed."

Each level should add a link in the chain, not simply more words.
