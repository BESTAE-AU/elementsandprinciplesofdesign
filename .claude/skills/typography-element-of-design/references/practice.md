# Typography: applying it in a design

Use this file when someone is designing something (their own project or a student's) and asks how to handle typography, what to change or how to justify it.

## Workflow

1. **Clarify the brief.** Audience, purpose, every output or touchpoint, production methods, and fixed constraints (brand rules, budget, equipment).
2. **Name the design question** before changing anything, e.g. "which weight keeps the mark readable at 16 px and in embroidery?". No random variation (`method/experimentation.md`).
3. **Pick one variable** from the Variables table in `SKILL.md` and propose three clearly different options. Hold the rest constant.
4. **Test in real conditions:** real size, real medium, real viewing distance or device. Check the production table and the field notes in `knowledge.md`.
5. **If it's a brand,** extend surviving options to at least three touchpoints and record what breaks away from the logo (`method/experimentation.md`).
6. **Decide and record** the result as a rule (minimum sizes, permitted variants) and as a sophisticated justification: evidence → alternative rejected → trade-off accepted (`method/justification.md`). The justification ladder is in `feedback.md`.
