# Proximity: examples and teaching material

## Common misconceptions

| Misconception | Better understanding |
|---|---|
| "Even spacing looks tidy." | Even spacing everywhere destroys grouping. |
| "Dividing lines are needed to separate content." | Gaps often separate better with less clutter. |
| "Proximity is just about neatness." | It communicates structure and meaning. |

## Worked analysis (model for exemplars and annotations)

Scenario: A registration form where each label sits 4 px above its field and 24 px below the previous field.

- **Identify:** "The design uses proximity."
- **Describe:** "Labels are much closer to their own field (4 px) than to the one above (24 px)."
- **Explain:** "Each label clearly belongs to the field below it."
- **Analyse:** "Proximity creates structure without boxes or lines; the ratio of gaps (4:24) makes grouping unambiguous."
- **Evaluate:** "Effective for fast, accurate completion. However, error messages appear 24 px below fields, as far away as the next label, so users may link them to the wrong field; moving them closer would fix it."

Each level should add a link in the chain, not simply more words.

## Justification examples

- **Weak:** "I spaced things out nicely."
- **Developing:** "I put labels close to their boxes so it's clear."
- **Strong:** "I placed each label 4 px above its field and 24 px from the previous one, so users can instantly tell which label belongs to which field."
- **Sophisticated:** "In testing, two users attached an error message to the wrong field because it sat midway. I moved errors to 4 px under the field, which made the form slightly longer but removed the confusion."

## Feedback patterns to look for in student work

- **Equal spacing everywhere.**
- **Headings closer to the text above** than to their own section.
- **Lines used instead of space.**
- **Ambiguous groups** in forms and diagrams.

## Activity ideas for teaching resources

- **Group by distance:** arrange scattered words into groups using only spacing.
- **Heading spacing:** fix headings that float between sections.
- **Form fix (UI):** regroup a confusing form.
- **Menu layout:** group canteen items using gaps only.
