## What contrast is

Contrast is the degree of difference between elements. Differences in value, colour, size, shape, texture, weight, direction or timing make things distinguishable, create emphasis and can carry meaning.

Keep it distinct:
- **Contrast vs emphasis:** contrast is the difference; emphasis is the resulting focal point.
- **Contrast vs variety:** variety is using different elements; contrast is how strongly they differ.
- **Value contrast vs colour contrast:** two very different hues can have almost the same value and still be hard to read.

## Types and variations

- **Value contrast:** light against dark; the most reliable for legibility.
- **Colour contrast:** hue, saturation or temperature differences.
- **Scale contrast:** large against small.
- **Shape and form contrast:** geometric against organic, sharp against soft.
- **Texture contrast:** rough against smooth, matte against gloss.
- **Typographic contrast:** weight, style, case or family differences.
- **Temporal contrast:** fast against slow, sound against silence.

## Levers: what a designer changes to create contrast

- Value
- Hue and saturation
- Size
- Shape
- Texture and finish
- Type weight and style
- Pace and timing

## What each element contributes

- **Colour:** hue, value and saturation contrast — value contrast is usually the most reliable.
- **Direction:** opposing directions create conflict, comparison or a sense of confrontation.
- **Form:** solid against void, geometric against organic, sharp against soft edges.
- **Layout and composition:** dense against open, large against small, image against text.
- **Line:** thick against thin, straight against curved, mechanical against gestural.
- **Shape:** a single organic shape among geometric ones, or one sharp shape among rounded ones, stands out.
- **Size and scale:** extreme scale differences create drama or surprise.
- **Space:** dense against open areas.
- **Texture:** rough against smooth, matte against gloss (e.g. a spot-gloss logo on matte packaging).
- **Time and duration:** a sudden change of pace (fast montage to a long still shot).
- **Typography:** serif against sans, heavy against light, large against small.
- **Value:** value contrast is the strongest and most reliable contrast.
