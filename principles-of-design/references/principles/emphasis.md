# Emphasis

## Definition

Emphasis is the intentional creation of prominence so that a particular element, area, message or moment receives increased attention.

## Creation strategies

- contrast
- isolation
- scale
- colour
- value
- unusual shape
- texture
- directional cues
- position
- movement
- timing
- sound
- interruption of repetition
- negative space

## Core logic

Emphasis depends on difference relative to context.

An element is not inherently emphasised; it becomes emphasised because it differs from surrounding conditions or occupies a strategically important position.

## Relationships

- contrast often creates emphasis
- emphasis can strengthen hierarchy
- disruption of repetition can create emphasis
- isolation can create emphasis through proximity and space
- movement can create temporal emphasis

## Applications

Branding: campaign message, product feature, call to action.  
Film: subject placement, lighting, focus, sound and editing.  
Motion graphics: timed entry, scale change, motion contrast.  
UI: primary buttons, warnings, selected states.  
Fashion: focal details, colour accents, silhouette features.  
Spatial design: focal walls, lighting, architectural features.

## Accessibility

Emphasis should not rely exclusively on colour, motion or subtle contrast. Urgent information must remain perceivable.

## Trade-offs

- strong focal point ↔ supporting information may be suppressed
- multiple emphases ↔ competition
- animation emphasis ↔ distraction or motion sensitivity
- visual dominance ↔ reduced balance

## Misconception

**Misconception:** Emphasis means making something large.  
**Correction:** Emphasis can be produced through many forms of difference, including isolation, contrast, movement or interruption.
